## 1. Plan Overview
- **Product name:** Bharti AXA Life Flexi Term Pro
- **Type:** A non-linked, non-participating, individual, pure risk premium life insurance plan (a term plan).
- **Purpose:** Provides life insurance coverage to secure your family's financial needs at an affordable price.
- **UIN:** 130N103V03

### Two plan options
1. **Without Return of Premium** — pure term cover (no money back on survival).
2. **With Return of Premium (ROP)** — returns the total premiums paid if you survive the term.

### Highlights
- **Options:** Without Return of Premium and With Return of Premium.
- **Single or Joint Life:** Under the *Without Return of Premium* variant you can cover a single life or **Joint Life** (cover for your spouse under the same policy).
- **Flexibility:** Choose from multiple policy terms and premium payment terms.
- **Whole Life Cover:** Option to stay covered up to age **99**.
- **Increase Sum Assured / Top-up:** Option to increase your cover as your life-stage needs grow.

## 4. Key Benefits

### a) Death Benefit
On death of the Life Insured during the policy term (policy in force and all due premiums paid), the Death Benefit is paid to the nominee/beneficiary immediately.

- **For Single Pay:** Sum Assured on Death is the **highest of** (1) 11 times the Single Premium, or (2) the absolute amount assured (the Sum Assured).
- **For Regular & Limited Pay:** Sum Assured on Death is the **highest of** (1) 11 times the Annualized Premium, (2) 105% of total premiums paid as on date of death, or (3) the absolute Sum Assured.

**Joint Life variant:**
- On death of the Primary Insured (Secondary alive): Death Benefit paid immediately; future premiums for the surviving Secondary Life are **waived**, and cover continues for the remaining term.
- On death of the Secondary Insured (Primary alive): Death Benefit paid immediately; the policy continues with premiums payable for the Primary Life.
- If the Secondary Life is a **non-working spouse**, the Sum Assured is **50% of the Primary's**, subject to a maximum of **Rs 50 lakh**. For a working spouse it can vary up to the Primary's Sum Assured.

### b) Death Benefit Payout options (chosen at inception)
1. **Lumpsum:** 100% of the Death Benefit paid immediately on death.
2. **Monthly Income:** Death Benefit paid as **1.04% of the Sum Assured every month for 10 years** (120 installments), first installment immediately on death. (Under One Year Term: **1.0%** of Sum Assured per month for 10 years.)
3. **Lumpsum plus Monthly Income:** 50% paid immediately as lumpsum; the remaining 50% paid as **0.93% of the total Sum Assured every month for 5 years**, first installment immediately on death.

The nominee may opt to take the monthly income as a lump sum (present value of monthly incomes discounted at 5% p.a.). Not available for One Year Policy Terms.

### c) Maturity Benefit — only for the Return of Premium option
If the Life Insured survives to maturity (all due premiums paid), the **Sum Assured on Maturity** is payable. It equals the **sum of total premiums paid** till the date of maturity. **No maturity benefit** is payable under the *Without Return of Premium* option.

### d) Increase Sum Assured during the term
Under "To Age" policy terms with Regular Pay or Limited Pay (Pay till Age 60), you may **increase the Sum Assured twice** during the term **without further medical examination**, provided the outstanding term is at least 10 years and your age is not over 45. Each increase = 50% of the Sum Assured at inception; cumulative increase capped at 100%. Must be chosen at inception. **Not** available under Joint Life or With Return of Premium options.

### e) Tax Benefits
You may be eligible for tax benefits on premiums paid and benefits received, as per prevailing tax laws (subject to change).

## 6. Premium Payment, Grace, Lapse & Revival
- **Grace period:** 15 days for monthly mode; 30 days for annual/semi-annual/quarterly modes (not applicable to Single Premium). The policy stays in force during the grace period.
- **Lapse:** If due premiums are not paid within the grace period, a *Without Return of Premium* policy lapses and cover ceases. (For Joint Life, specific rules apply to primary/secondary lives.)
- **Revival:** A lapsed policy can be revived within **5 years** of the first unpaid premium due date, by paying all outstanding premiums plus interest. Revival interest rate for FY 24-25 is **7.82% p.a.**

## 7. Surrender Values
**Without Return of Premium:** Pays an **Unexpired Risk Premium**.
- Limited Pay: acquired after payment of 2 annualized premiums. Single Pay: acquired immediately. **No** unexpired risk premium for Regular Pay or One Year Term.
- Formula: Unexpired Risk Premium = 70% × P × (U/T) × (P/PP), where P = premiums paid, U = outstanding term (months), T = policy term (months), PP = total premiums payable.

**With Return of Premium:** Acquires a surrender value after the first policy year (one full year premium received). On surrender, the **higher of the Special Surrender Value (SSV) or Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV)** is paid.

**Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) factors — Single Pay** (as % of premiums paid):
| Premiums Paid | GSV Factor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | 75% |
| 4 and above | 90% |

**GSV factors — Regular & Limited Pay** (as % of premiums paid):
| Premiums Paid | GSV Factor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0% |
| 2 | 30% |
| 3 | 35% |
| 4 to 7 | 50% |
| 8 and above | 51% to 90% |

Special Surrender Values are non-guaranteed and may change subject to IRDAI approval.

## 8. Paid-Up Benefit (Return of Premium option only)
After the first policy year (one full year premium paid), if further premiums aren't paid within the grace period, the policy becomes **Paid Up** with reduced benefits:
- **Death:** Paid-Up Sum Assured on Death = (No. of premiums received / No. of premiums payable) × Sum Assured on Death.
- **Maturity:** Paid-Up Sum Assured on Maturity = (premiums received / premiums payable) × Sum Assured on Maturity.
- **Surrender:** higher of SSV or GSV.

## 10. Policy Loans
- Available **only** under the *Return of Premium on Maturity* option, once the policy has acquired surrender value.
- **Minimum loan:** Rs 15,000. **Maximum loan:** 70% of the surrender value.
- Loan interest for FY 24-25: **10.18% p.a. simple interest** (10-year G-Sec rate on 1 April + 3%, reviewed annually).

## 11. Important Terms & Conditions
- **Free-look period:** 30 days from receipt of the policy document to return it if you disagree with the terms; premium is refunded after proportionate deductions.
- **Suicide exclusion:** On death due to suicide within 12 months of commencement or revival, the nominee receives at least **80% of total premiums paid** (or the surrender value, if higher), provided the policy is in force.
- **Assignment & Nomination:** As per Sections 38 and 39 of the Insurance Act, 1938.

## 12. Case Study Examples
- **Mr. Reddy (35, non-smoker):** Single Life, Without ROP, To Age 85, Regular Pay, Lumpsum payout, Sum Assured Rs 1 crore for **Rs 20,200 p.a.** (excl. taxes). On death, family receives Rs 1 crore. At 40 he increases cover by 50% to Rs 1.5 crore for an extra Rs 12,550 p.a. (total Rs 32,750 p.a.).
- **Nikhil (40) & Neha (35, homemaker):** Joint Life, Without ROP, 35-year term, Regular Pay, Lumpsum. Sum Assured Rs 1 crore for Nikhil and Rs 50 lakh for Neha, total premium **Rs 30,850** (excl. taxes). On Nikhil's death, Neha gets Rs 1 crore and her future premiums are waived; on Neha's death, Nikhil gets Rs 50 lakh and continues paying.
- **Krutika (40, non-smoker):** With Return of Premium, 25-year term, Regular Pay, Sum Assured Rs 1 crore for **Rs 35,600 p.a.** On death, family gets Rs 1 crore. On survival to age 65, she gets **Rs 8,90,000** (all premiums) back as lumpsum.
